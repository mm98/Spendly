The heading over a set of rows that carries a total: the label starts on the rows' first column and the total ends on their amounts.

## Props

- `label`: the heading, drawn as a section label
- `amount`: the total

## Rules

- Takes no `className` or `style`.
- Sits directly above `ListItem` rows, on their inset.

## Example

```jsx
<ListItemHead label="Items · 3" amount="12.749,00 kr." />
```
