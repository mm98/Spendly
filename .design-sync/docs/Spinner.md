A small turning ring for work of unknown length, on one line of text or inside a mark that is uploading.

## Props

None. It takes no props.

## Rules

- Takes no `className` or `style`.
- It is hidden from screen readers: the text beside it says what is happening ("Reading the receipt").

## Example

```jsx
<div className="sp-row-flex sp-gap-8">
	<Spinner />
	<span className="sp-subtitle">Reading the receipt</span>
</div>
```
