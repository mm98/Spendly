- You can turn on two-step sign-in in Settings, Account and profile, with an app on your phone.
  A password alone can be guessed or stolen. A code from your phone keeps your account safer.
- Signing in then asks for a 6-digit code after your password, or one of your recovery codes.
  So a stolen password is not enough to get in.
- You get an email when two-step sign-in is turned on or off, or when a recovery code is used.
  So you know at once if someone else changes how your account is protected.
