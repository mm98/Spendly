- On Orders, Reconcile checks your bank's file and shows the payments that have no order.
  You wanted to know if every expense is registered in Spendly.
- Pick a vendor for a payment with no order, and Create makes the order from the bank's line.
  The bank text does not always name the shop, so you choose it.
- Reconcile reads an Excel file as well as a CSV file, on Budget and on Orders.
  Your bank can export both, and the columns you set are kept for each.
