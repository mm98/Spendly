- On Orders, Reconcile has two sliders: Days apart and Amount difference, from 0 to 10.
  Your bank can book a payment days later, or with a small difference, and you set how much to allow.
- A payment close to its order's amount shows as Registered, with the difference: "0.38 kr. more".
  The bank's amount is always right, so you see which order is a little off.
