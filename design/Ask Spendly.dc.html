24a Ask Spendly - a conversation
A page for asking about your own money in plain words, opened from its sidebar row above Settings. Answers are read from the reader's orders, vendors, categories, budget and statistics; nothing can be changed from here. One conversation, kept for the visit, across the full content column, with the question field pinned under it and the reader's saved questions above the field. Clear and Email this chat sit in the page's controls bar.
Notes for implementation
Route and entry. /ask. The sidebar row Ask Spendly (message-circle) sits below the divider, directly above Settings, on every sidebar (9a); on the rail it is the icon with the tooltip. G then A goes here, listed in the shortcuts sheet and as a Go to destination in the palette. The palette has no ask state: a question is only asked here. On a phone it is a row in More, above Settings (Mobile screens 7a). When the server has no assistant set up, the row, the shortcut and the route are absent.
The conversation takes the full content column, the same width as the controls bar above it, as every page's content does. The thread scrolls in .sp-chat__scroll; the dock under it never scrolls. Opening the page, and every new message, scrolls to the latest one.
A message is a mark and a bubble (.sp-chat__msg), the mark outside the bubble on its first line. The reader's question is --mine: their own account mark, the one the sidebar's account row shows (initials on its swatch, or the profile photo as sp-mark--photo), at the right, with the question in a --green-tint bubble beside it. Spendly's answer is at the left with the Spendly mark, the sidebar brand's solid "S", in a bubble on --surface with the --line border; a table sits inside it. While an answer comes, its bubble holds the spinner. A message is at most 75% of the column wide, whatever it holds, a table included, so the two sides always read apart.
Answers are text, a short list, or a small table in .sp-chat__figures (the compact static table, framed). Every amount is .sp-amount (the Money component), in a sentence as in a cell. A named order, vendor or category is a link to its record; it opens in the same tab, and Back returns to the conversation as it was.
The field is a textarea one control high, growing to six lines and then scrolling. Enter sends, Shift Enter makes a new line, Send is disabled while the field is empty. It takes focus when the page opens. Follow-ups are sent with the earlier questions and answers of this conversation.
Kept for the visit. Leaving the page and coming back in the same visit shows the conversation as it was. A reload or signing out ends it; nothing is stored on the server.

Notes for implementation
1 - Nothing asked yet, nothing saved is the empty state (c13) with the three example questions under it as small outline buttons; choosing one sends it. Email this chat and Clear are disabled.
2 - While an answer comes the question shows at once, the answer's place shows the spinner and "Reading your orders", and Send becomes Stop in its place. Stop ends the request and leaves the question in the thread. The field stays typable; Enter does nothing until the answer is in or stopped. Email this chat is disabled until the answer is in.
3 - The day's limit shows only near its end. With 3 or fewer left, the hint line states the count in sp-meta--over at its right.
4 - None left: the amber alert closes the thread and states when asking opens again, from the server's reset time; the field is disabled with the placeholder "No questions left today". The conversation can still be read, emailed and cleared.
5 - A failed answer stays where it would have been, in --red-ink, with Try again, which sends the same question again in place. A failed answer does not count against the limit. A conversation whose last answer failed cannot be emailed until it is answered or cleared.
6 - Saved questions. Each question the reader sends carries a small bookmark button beside its bubble (.sp-chat__save, aria-pressed); pressing it saves the question as it was typed, and the bookmark fills in green. Pressing it again removes it. Saved questions are kept on the server for the account, across visits and devices, at most 8, newest last. At 8 the bookmark on an unsaved question is disabled, with the tooltip "8 saved. Remove one to save another." Saving the same wording twice is not possible: the bookmark on a question already saved shows it as saved.
The saved row (.sp-chat__saved) sits above the field whenever something is saved, on the empty page and in a conversation alike: one small outline button per saved question, the same button the examples use, and Edit. Choosing one sends it at once, as an example does. Once anything is saved, the empty page shows no examples: the saved row takes their place. On a phone the row is one line that scrolls sideways.
7 - Edit turns the saved questions into removable chips (c6, the static chip with its remove button) and Edit into Done. Removing one takes effect at once, with no undo and no confirm: it is one line the reader can save again from any question. There is no reorder and no rename; the wording is the question. Done, Esc, or sending a question ends editing.

Notes for implementation
Clear empties the conversation at once and returns the page to state 1, with the toast "Conversation cleared" and Undo for the toast's usual time (c18). Undo puts every message back as it was. It is disabled while nothing has been asked, and while an answer is coming. A cleared conversation is not sent anywhere.
Email this chat sends the whole conversation to the account's own address, with one press and no dialog, and the toast names the address. It is disabled while nothing has been answered, while an answer is coming, and when the last answer failed.
It cannot be pressed again and again. While the send is in flight the button is disabled and reads "Sending" with the spinner. Once sent it reads Emailed and stays disabled until the conversation changes: a new answer, Clear, or Undo. The server also takes at most one conversation email per account per minute; a send it refuses shows the problem toast "Wait a minute before emailing this chat again." and the button returns to Email this chat.
On a phone both sit in the top bar's menu (Mobile screens 7a), with the same states: Email this chat is disabled while sending and reads Emailed after.
