24a Ask - a conversation
A page for asking about your own money in plain words, opened from its sidebar row above Settings. Answers are read from the reader's orders, vendors, categories, budget and statistics; nothing can be changed from here. One chat at a time across the full content column, with the question field pinned under it and the conversation menu beside Send. Every chat is kept for 30 days after its last message, on Chats (24f).
Notes for implementation
Route and entry. /ask. The sidebar row Ask (message-circle) sits below the divider, directly above Settings, on every sidebar (9a); on the rail it is the icon with the tooltip. G then A goes here, listed in the shortcuts sheet and as a Go to destination in the palette. The palette has no ask state: a question is only asked here. On a phone it is a row in More, above Settings (Mobile screens 7a). On a window under 560px tall, where Settings moves into the account menu, Ask moves with it, as the row directly above Settings (9c, 9g). When the server has no assistant set up, the row, the shortcut and the route are absent.
The conversation takes the full content column, as every page's content does. The page has no controls bar: the conversation starts directly under the top bar. The thread scrolls in .sp-chat__scroll; the dock under it never scrolls. Opening the page, and every new message, scrolls to the latest one.
A message is a mark and a bubble (.sp-chat__msg), the mark outside the bubble on its first line. The reader's question is --mine: their own account mark, the one the sidebar's account row shows (initials on its swatch, or the profile photo as sp-mark--photo), at the right, with the question in a --green-tint bubble beside it. Spendly's answer is at the left with the Spendly mark, the sidebar brand's solid "S", in a bubble on --surface with the --line border; a table sits inside it. While an answer comes, its bubble holds the spinner. A message is at most 75% of the column wide, so the two sides read apart. On touch, an answer that holds a table takes the full column instead (Mobile screens 7a), so a phone has room for its three columns.
Answers are text, a short list, a small table in .sp-chat__figures (the compact static table, framed), a file to download or a chart (24d). The conversation is the page's body size (--t-13): questions, answers, and in an answer every table cell and every amount, names, counts and amounts alike; an amount keeps its weight. The table's head keeps its label size. The table sizes its number and amount columns to their content, on one line, and every text column shares the rest and wraps when it is longer. Nothing in the table is cut, whichever column comes first. A name is the link to its record. Every amount is .sp-amount (the Money component), in a sentence as in a cell. A named order, vendor or category is a link to its record; it opens in the same tab, and Back returns to the conversation as it was.
The field is a textarea one control high, growing to six lines and then scrolling. Enter sends, Shift Enter makes a new line, Send is disabled while the field is empty. It takes focus when the page opens on a desktop; on a phone it does not, so the keyboard does not cover the conversation. The placeholder reads "Ask about your orders, vendors and budget"; on a phone it reads "Ask a follow-up" once something is asked (7a). An empty field is one line: a placeholder longer than the field is cut with ... rather than wrapping it to two lines. Follow-ups are sent with the earlier questions and answers of this conversation.
Every chat is kept, on the server for the account, for 30 days after its last message, and is listed on Chats (24f). Asking in a reopened chat starts its 30 days again, and the follow-up is sent with the whole chat before it. A chat is created with its first question and has its own address, /ask/:chatId, so Back, a reload and the emailed chat's button (20f) open it again. /ask shows the chat that was open last in this visit; a new visit, and New chat (24c), open the empty page (24b 1). A file a chat made is kept for 30 days from when it was made, on Files (24e).

Notes for implementation
1 - Nothing asked yet is the empty state (c13) with the reader's saved questions under it. Its line says that an answer can be a file or a chart; the full list is behind the button in the field (24c). A new account starts with three: "How much did I spend on food in August?", "Which shop did I use most this year?" and "Make a spreadsheet of my orders this month", the last one showing that an answer can be a file (24d). They are saved questions like any other, so they count toward the 8 and can be removed. Accounts that exist already get them once, skipping any wording already saved and only as many as fit under 8. Choosing one sends it. In the conversation menu, Email this chat and New chat are disabled (24c).
2 - While an answer comes the question shows at once, the answer's place shows the spinner and "Reading your orders", and Send becomes Stop in its place. Stop ends the request and leaves the question in the thread. The field stays typable; Enter does nothing until the answer is in or stopped. Email this chat is disabled until the answer is in.
3 - The day's limit shows only near its end. With 3 or fewer left, the hint line states the count in sp-meta--over at its right. On a phone the hint line has no keys: it shows only this count, at the right under the field, and is absent otherwise.
4 - None left: the amber alert closes the thread and states when asking opens again, from the server's reset time; the field is disabled with the placeholder "No questions left today". The chat can still be read and emailed, and New chat still opens the empty page.
5 - A failed answer stays where it would have been, in --red-ink, with Try again, which sends the same question again in place. A failed answer does not count against the limit. A conversation whose last answer failed cannot be emailed until it is answered.
6 - Saved questions. Each question the reader sends carries a small bookmark button beside its bubble (.sp-chat__save, aria-pressed); pressing it saves the question as it was typed, and the bookmark fills in green. Pressing it again removes it. Saved questions are kept on the server for the account, across visits and devices, at most 8, newest last. At 8 the bookmark on an unsaved question is disabled, with the tooltip "8 saved. Remove one to save another." Saving the same wording twice is not possible: the bookmark on a question already saved shows it as saved.
On the empty page the saved questions sit under the empty state (.sp-chat__examples), oldest first: each is a small outline button that sends it at once, with its small remove button beside it (.sp-chat__pick). Removing one takes effect at once, with no undo and no confirm, and a removed example does not come back: it is one line the reader can save again from any question. There is no Edit, no reorder and no rename; the wording is the question. A saved question is one line, at most 320px wide and never wider than the column; a longer one is cut with ... and shows the whole question as its tooltip. It is kept as typed, so a cut one still sends in full. A phone shows the same list, the same way.
7 - Nothing saved: once every saved question is removed, the empty state stands alone with one line under it in sp-meta: "Save a question with the bookmark beside it, and it shows here."
In a conversation nothing sits above the field, and the saved questions are not offered: they are on the empty page only, and New chat returns to it. A phone does the same.

    One button sits to the right of Send, --sp-8 from it as Send is from the field: the outline icon button at the field's height (.sp-chat__more, dots-vertical), with the tooltip "More" lined up with its right side and the label "Conversation menu". It opens the Menu (c9) upward, 6 clear of the button, its right side on the button's right side, and it closes on a choice, Escape or a click outside; the arrow keys move through it. Clear is gone: New chat takes its place.
    The items, in this order: Chats (message-circle) opens 24f; Files (paperclip) opens 24e; Email this chat (mail); a rule; New chat (plus). Chats and Files are links and are never disabled.
    New chat opens the empty page at once (24b 1), with no toast and no undo, since nothing is lost: the chat that was open stays on Chats as it was. It is disabled while nothing has been asked and while an answer is coming.
    Email this chat sends the whole conversation to the account's own address, with one press and no dialog; the menu closes and the toast names the address. It is disabled while nothing has been answered, while an answer is coming, and when the last answer failed. When the server cannot send email it is absent, as every email feature is.
    It cannot be pressed again and again. While the send is in flight the item reads "Sending", disabled, with the spinner in place of its icon. Once sent it reads Emailed with the check, and stays disabled until the conversation changes: a new answer or another chat. The server also takes at most one conversation email per account per minute; a send it refuses shows the problem toast "Wait a minute before emailing this chat again." and the item returns to Email this chat.
    On Chats and Files the button is Back to chat. Both pages keep the chat bar at the bottom, and in the menu's place, at the same size, sits the outline icon link with chevron-left, tooltip and label "Back to chat". It opens the chat open last in this visit, or Ask's empty page when none was (24b 1). So the way back is where the reader pressed to leave, and the page has no Back at its top. A question asked on Chats or Files goes to that same chat and opens it there; with none open this visit it starts a new chat.
    What Ask can do. The chat field holds one icon button at its right edge (.sp-chat__wrap around the field, the trail button .sp-field__trail with circle-help), tooltip and label "What Ask can do". It sits on the field's last line, level with Send, as the field grows. It is the field's own part, so it is wherever the field is: the empty page, a conversation, Chats and Files (24e, 24f) and the phone (Mobile screens 7a). It is never disabled, also when the field is. No key is bound to it: "?" stays the keyboard shortcuts everywhere, and typing "?" in the field types it.
    It opens the Dialog (c13) over the page, with the heading "What Ask can do", the parts in .sp-chat__help, and in the actions row the footnote "Files are kept for 30 days" (sp-note) at the left and Close at the right. The footnote is absent when the server makes no files. Escape, Close and a click on the scrim close it, and focus returns to the button. A dialog taller than the window scrolls its parts; the heading and Close stay. On a phone the same parts open in the bottom sheet (Mobile screens 7a), with the footnote over Close, which is a block button under them.
    Two parts: Files, then Charts. Each is .sp-chat__help-part: the section label, its formats on one line, comma separated (.sp-chat__formats), wrapping when long, and one example question. Nothing else is in the help: what Ask reads stays on the empty page, and the limits stay under the field.
    Files and Charts are filled from the server's list of what Ask makes, sent with GET /api/assistant: one part per kind, in the server's order, the kind's name as the label, its formats in one line, joined with ", " in the server's order and with the names the server gives them. A new kind is a new part and a new format one more name in the line, with no change to the design. Each kind carries one example question. A kind with no formats is not shown.
    An example question is a saved question's button without its remove button (.sp-chat__pick), so it is one line, at most 320px, cut with ... and with the whole question as its tooltip. Pressing it closes the dialog and puts the question in the field with the cursor at its end, replacing what was typed; it is not sent, so the reader can change "October" before sending. Saved questions on the empty page still send at once (24b 6).
    The help states no limits: the questions left show under the field (24b 3, 4), as before. With no questions left the example buttons are disabled, since the field is. When the server makes no files, the Files part stays with the line "Ask cannot make files on this Spendly." and no list or example.
    On a phone the top bar's menu holds the same four items in the same order, with the same states (Mobile screens 7a).

    Notes for implementation
    An answer is blocks, in the order written: text, a list, a table, files, a chart, and text again. One answer can hold several of each. The files of one block share one frame (.sp-chat__files), one row per file (.sp-chat__file), a rule between rows, in the order they were made. A chart sits in the figure frame a table uses (.sp-chat__figures), holding .sp-chat__chart. On touch, an answer holding files or a chart takes the full column, as one holding a table does.
    A file row is the document mark (file-text on sp-mark--medium sp-mark--none), the title, a second line in sp-meta, then Download: the outline icon button with download, the tooltip "Download" and the label "Download" plus the title. The title is the question as it was sent, and nothing else: "Make a spreadsheet of my orders at Bilka this year". Two files from one question share the title and are told apart by their type. The title is one line, cut with ..., the whole of it as the tooltip; on touch, where the row has less room beside its buttons, it takes two lines before it is cut. The second line is the type, Excel, Word, PDF or CSV, and the size, in KB under 1 MB and in MB with one decimal above. The downloaded file is named after the heading at the top of the file, then the time it was made in brackets, in the account's time zone, the time written with full stops so it saves on every system: "Orders at Bilka in 2026 (2026-10-22 14.05.33).xlsx". The row keeps the question as its title; the time is only in the file name, never on the row.
    Only the signed-in owner can download. Download on a signed-out tab opens sign-in, then the download. A file is kept for 30 days from when it was made, and is listed on Files (24e) for those days.
    2 - While a file is made the answer's place shows the spinner bubble as in 24b 2, with "Making your spreadsheet", "Making your document", "Making your PDF" or "Making your CSV"; Stop works as before and ends the answer and the file. 3 - When the text arrives before a file is ready, the row shows the spinner in Download's place, disabled, and its second line reads the type and "Being made". The row becomes ready in place.
    4 - A file that could not be made keeps its row, the second line in --red-ink (.sp-chat__failed) stating why in one line, and no Download. The text of the answer still arrives. The two reasons are too much data ("too many orders for one file. Ask for one year at a time.") and the day's limit on files ("today's files are used up. Ask again tomorrow.").
    5 - A file that is gone keeps its row, so the answer still shows it was there: the title in --ink-4 ([data-state="gone"]), no Download, and the second line reads the type and "Removed after 30 days", or "Deleted" when the reader deleted it on Files.
    A chart is the library's own Bars and Donut (c14) at their own sizes, no new chart: Bars at its 140 height with no axis, the Donut at 128 beside its shares. Above it, the chart's name in sp-section-label and the period or total in sp-meta (.sp-chat__chart-name). The amounts are in the readout and the shares, and the answer's own text states the figure that matters. A chart with more than one series has the legend under it, at most four series on the four keys. Bars over time take up to 12 columns; the five biggest of something are shown as the top three and Other, on the four keys, as Statistics does.
    6 - The readout is the chart tooltip (sp-tip--panel): the step's full name and amount, then the order count, or one row per series with its key. It shows on hover, on keyboard focus (each column takes focus and is labelled "August 2026, 9.840,00 kr., 31 orders"), and on a tap on touch, where a tap elsewhere closes it. It hangs above its bar, centred; the first and last columns line it up with their own edge. A column does not open anything, so it takes no hand and no lit bar. A share row is a link to its category, opening in the same tab; Other opens Categories.
    A chart has no Download. The numbers behind it are a file the reader can ask for. In the email it is an image (20f).
    9 - While a chart is made the bubble reads "Drawing your chart". 10 - A chart that could not be made keeps its frame and name, with one line in --red-ink saying why and what to ask instead; the text of the answer still arrives.
    On a phone's 290px bubble a chart fits with no sideways scroll: 12 bars at the 8 gap are about 14px wide, and where two ticks would touch every second one is left empty, as on every chart. The Donut moves above its shares when the bubble is narrower than the two side by side.

    Search filters the list as the reader types, over the headings and the text of every question and answer, keeping the order. Clearing the field shows the whole list again. When nothing matches, the list is the empty state "No chats found" with "Try another word, or clear the search."
    Delete acts at once, with no dialog: the row leaves the list, and the chat and every file its answers made are deleted. The toast names what went, "Chat deleted" or "Chat and its 2 files deleted", with Undo for the toast's usual time (c18), which brings back both. Deleting the chat that is open in Ask returns Ask to its empty page.
    Kept for 30 days after its last message. Past that the chat leaves the list. An address of a chat that was deleted or is past its 30 days, from Back, a bookmark or an emailed chat, opens Ask's empty page with the problem toast "That chat is no longer kept." Only the signed-in owner can open a chat; signed out, the address opens sign-in, then the chat.
    No chats is the empty state (c13) with the message-circle mark: "No chats yet" and "Ask Spendly something, and the chat is kept here for 30 days after its last message."
