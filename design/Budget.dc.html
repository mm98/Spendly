21a Budget - month by month
The budget is the yearly plan: rows in groups, each with one amount expected every month and the real amount of each month as it happens. It opens on the month it is now and the one after it. The example is built from a real bank export for January to October 2026. Its bills are paid automatically from the Budget account; Salary comes into the Salary account and is typed in by hand each month. The month in front is read on the dark card; every month below it is a panel of its own, and an amount is typed straight into it.

Notes for implementation
Every part is an existing component. The bar is the controls bar (c20). The dark card is Home 10a's month card: the same stat row and the same gauge with its plan marker (c10, c14). The month stepper is the period control's window (c14) and the twelve months under it are the full-width Segmented (c5, --facet). Each month is a well (c26) of summary rows (c21).
The year is a Segmented while there are four years or fewer. From the fifth it becomes a Combobox, the rule every set in the library follows. The months are the one exception: twelve is fixed and ordered, never a list that grows, so all twelve stay visible as segments across the card.
Below 600px one month is shown, and the segments page one month at a time. The window label then reads the one month.
The gauge is costs over income. The marker is the same share computed from the expected amounts. Where no row has an expected amount the marker and the second half of the line are absent. Costs with no income count as 100%.
Under the pointer the whole line takes the grey --hover ground, corner to corner at --r-tile, as Salary is drawn, and does the same in edit mode. Keyboard focus keeps the --ring; hover and focus can show together. On a phone nothing changes.
Clicking an amount turns it into a NumberField in place, the same input as 21b. Enter saves and moves down, Tab saves and moves right, Escape cancels. Typing a digit on a focused amount starts the edit with that digit.
A row's tag is computed: Varies when its months differ, Only Mar when it falls in three months or fewer, Not every month otherwise. A row that is the same every month has none.
Grey is an expected amount with no real amount yet. The real amount replaces it as soon as one is typed. Grey uses sp-ink-5; there is no budget-specific colour.

Notes for implementation
The sheet is the table (c1) in its compact, static form. Rows are not opened, so the lit row and the hand are off; each amount cell takes focus on its own. Number columns are sp-col-number. It is page markup, not a component: nothing else in the app is a grid of this shape.
Where the year does not fit, it scrolls sideways and opens on the month it is now. The sheet scrolls inside its card, and the month header and the row names stay in view in both directions; a section or group label holds still at the left. The month it is now is the accent ink in the header.
Under the pointer an amount cell takes the grey --hover ground inside its own box, as the first cell is drawn; it has no border until it is focused, and focus replaces the grey with the field's white, border and ring. On a phone nothing changes.
Every amount is a NumberField (c3) in its cell state: it reads as the figure until it is clicked or focused, then takes the field's border and ring inside its own cell, so nothing moves. It has no visible label - its accessible name is the real amount, the row and the month. A month with only an expected amount shows it as the grey placeholder. Electricity in October is drawn focused.
Arrows move between cells, Home and End go to January and December, Delete clears the real amount. Only the real amount is typed in a cell. The expected amount is one figure for the whole row, set in the row editor (21c).
Costs by account appears under Totals only when cost rows use two or more accounts. It lists each account in the accounts list's order, then No account if any cost row has none. An account set to Greyed out in the totals draws its row in sp-ink-5. With one account, as here, it is absent. Left over is green above zero and red below it.
21c Editing the budget
Amounts are typed at all times. Everything that changes the budget's shape - rows, groups, accounts and years - waits behind Edit, because it is done a few times a year. In edit mode a name opens its editor in place, every group ends in a row that adds to it, and the accounts list appears under the months.

Notes for implementation
The editor is the line editor in its plain form (c15, --plain), the one an order line opens, without the photo. A row is four fields in two pairs: its name and the amount expected each month, then its account and group. Real amounts are not in it - they are typed in the cells, here and in 21b. It takes the line editor's frame (c15) and its action row: the key hint at the left, Cancel and the commit at the right.
It opens in place of the row it edits, in the months view and in the whole year alike. In the whole year it spans the table's width and stays in view when the sheet is scrolled sideways.
Move up and Move down are disabled at the ends, and focus goes to the other one. Delete asks first, with the dialog from c13: the row, with every amount in it.
The add rows are AddRow (c33), the add control for any set that is not order lines. Each group ends in one, each side ends in one for a new group, and the accounts list ends in one.
Delete 2026 is the card footer's destructive button, the same as Delete on a vendor or a category (c10): solid red in a committing footer, and as the footer's only button it fills it. It asks first, stating the number of rows.
Under each account is what goes through it in the year being viewed: the monthly average of the income rows on it, or of the cost rows, or both on two lines. An account no row uses that year says so. The list itself is shared by every year; only this line changes with the year.
The accounts list appears under the months card while editing, and only then, since accounts are set up once and shared by every year. A name opens its editor in place (21d), and the list ends in an add row.

Notes for implementation
Both are the plain line editor with one field (sp-line-editor__grid--one). A new group or account has no move or delete, and its commit reads Add.
Two accounts cannot share a name, since the rows and the totals tell them apart by it. The message is the field's danger message: You already have an account called Budget.
Deleting an account counts its rows across every year in the dialog, since the list is shared. Its rows are left with no account.
Accounts reorder by dragging the handle, or with the keyboard from the handle. The order is the order of Costs by account in the totals.
An open account has no grip, as an open order line has none: it cannot be dragged while open. The handle column stays, empty, so the name keeps its line. Move up and Move down in the head are how an open account moves.
21e Starting a new year
New year starts the year after the newest one as a copy of it, with the same groups and rows. The one choice is what happens to the amounts.
Start 2027? 2027 starts as a copy of 2026, with the same groups and rows. Choose what happens to the amounts.
Every amount is copied as an expected amount, shown grey. Write the real amount as each month happens.
Notes for implementation
The dialog is c13 unchanged: the question, what it does, then Cancel at the left and the commit at the right.
The note under the Segmented changes with the choice. As they are: Every amount is copied as it is. Then clear the ones you do not know yet, like your salary, and write what you expect instead. No amounts: The groups and rows are copied, and every month is left empty.
Afterwards the page opens on 2027 and a toast says 2027 is ready.

Notes for implementation
Use TV antenna places the payment on the suggested row and marks it saved.
Pick from budget opens a searchable list of the budget's rows; picking one places the payment there.
Always leave out hides this text in every future file; Leave out once hides it for this file only.
Each side's heading is the list head (sp-list-head): inset like the rows, so Costs starts on the boxes and its total ends on the amounts. The same three parts as the months view: the controls bar, the dark card and its stats and gauge, then cards. Each payment line is the list row (c15) in its top-aligned form, with its actions as small outline buttons (c6), so they read as controls on the row rather than floating text.
Money coming in from another of your accounts is a transfer, not income. The monthly Fast overførsel is placed once as Always left out, so it never asks again; income is typed in the budget by hand. A payment refunded the same day, like Codan forsikring and the Overførsel back, is left out once on both sides.
The file is read on the device and never sent. Only the choices are saved: a text placed on a row, or always left out, and a payment left out once.
Show is a Segmented of four. To place lists the texts no row accounts for; Other amount, the payments found on a row where the budget holds another amount; Left out, what was left out; All, every payment with its status.
A note under a payment takes the ink of what it says: sp-ink-warning for a suggestion or an amount about to be replaced, sp-ink-danger for a year with no budget, sp-ink-success once a choice is saved. It is sp-note with an ink utility, not a new state.
A placed text stays in the list, marked saved, until the next file, so its choice can be undone where it was made.
Selecting texts replaces the hint with the bulk bar (c12): Put them all on, Always leave out, Leave out once. Put them all on is offered only when every selected text is money out, or every one is money in.
Save 6 amounts is the card footer's single button, so it fills the footer. Its count follows the ticks.

Notes for implementation
Close columns returns to the payment list with the chosen mapping.
Another file opens the file picker; the new file replaces the current one, and payments already placed stay placed.
The mapping is the table (c1), static, with a pick-only Combobox (c5) per column. It is page markup, not a component.
Each Spendly role is taken by one column at most: Date, Text, Amount, and Money out as its own column. Picking a role for a column takes it from the column that had it.
When every date in the file reads two ways, a second Combobox under Date asks which is right. This file writes 02-10-2026, and a date like 30-09-2026 can only be day first, so it is not asked. When the dates cannot be read, the date row carries the danger message: These are not dates Spendly can read.
A file with no header row is read from its first line. The columns are then named by number, and the samples are the first three payments. Columns 8 to 10 all hold dates; the booking date is the one taken. Columns that identify an account, a person or a payment are shown here as a description rather than their values.
The choice is kept in this browser, per column count. Closing the columns accepts them.
