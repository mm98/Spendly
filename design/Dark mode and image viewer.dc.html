12a Dark mode - warm dark
Not an inversion. The light theme's identity is warmth - a greige canvas, not grey - so the dark theme is a warm brown-black, and the greens lift rather than darken so they still read as the accent. Every token maps one-to-one, so a component references a role and never a literal.

Three more roles change and cannot be shown as a swatch: --ring becomes a 3px #1f3328 halo rather than the light theme's --green-tint, and --shadow-menu and --shadow-sheet keep their geometry but raise their opacity - 0.4 and 0.45 against 0.08 and 0.12 - because a shadow on a dark ground has less to darken.
Four rules behind the mapping: the sidebar does not change, because it was already dark and is the one thing that anchors the two themes. Elevation reverses - a light theme lifts a surface by shadow, a dark theme lifts it by getting lighter, so cards are lighter than the canvas and dialogs lighter again. Shadows stay, for layering only, with their opacity raised as the token table gives. And the chart ramp keeps its four steps in the same order: #5d9b76, #508062, #405e49, #313e32 - --green to --green-5 in the dark tokens.

    Those two blocks are the pages themselves. The same markup as Order editing 1b, Order list 2b and Statistics 4a, with data-theme="dark" on the frame around them.     The sidebar - light and dark, side by side
    The sidebar is the one element that does not participate in theming. It was already dark, and it is what anchors the two themes to each other: switching theme should not move the furniture. Its action, brand tile, account mark and warning counts take --shell-* literals for the same reason.

    Why it does not change
    That is the reason it anchors the themes. It was already dark, so switching theme leaves the furniture where it is: the destination list, the current item, the account row and the counts do not move or change weight. Only the ground they sit on does.
    It also means the sidebar keeps literal values rather than roles - #14261c for its ground, #c3d2c8 for a label at rest, #1f3d2c for the current destination. They are in tokens.css as --shell-*, outside the theme block, so a theme cannot reach them by accident.

    Notes for implementation
    Two things happen to images on dark. Every thumbnail keeps a 1px #463f37 border, because a white receipt scan with no border floats as a bright rectangle with no edge. And every thumbnail renders at --fade-photo, receipts, scans and files included, taking the glare off without touching colour accuracy - full strength returns on hover and in the viewer, where the image is the subject. The strength is light's; the list is not. Light tones photos of goods only and keeps them toned on hover (Images and files 17b), because there the tone evens out colour rather than glare. The two lists differ on purpose.
    The tiles above follow the page's own theme, rather than being drawn once for each. Only the sidebar pair in 12a is pinned, because there the comparison is the subject.
