12a Dark mode — warm dark
Not an inversion. The light theme's identity is warmth — a greige canvas, not grey — so the dark theme is a warm brown-black, and the greens lift rather than darken so they still read as the accent. Every token maps one-to-one, so a component references a role and never a literal.

Four rules behind the mapping: the sidebar does not change, because it was already dark and is the one thing that anchors the two themes. Elevation reverses — a light theme lifts a surface by shadow, a dark theme lifts it by getting lighter, so cards are lighter than the canvas and dialogs lighter again. Shadows stay but only for layering, at half the opacity of the light theme. And the chart ramp keeps its five steps in the same order, lifted: #5d9b76, #7fb392, #a3c9b0, #4a6b57, #39443c.
