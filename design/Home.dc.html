10a Home
The screen you land on. It answers three things without scrolling: where this month stands against the month you compare with, what needs your attention, and what you last recorded. Capture is the largest thing on the page, since adding a receipt is the most common reason to open the app.
Note on the frame
The rounded corners, the 1px outline and the drop shadow around the shell below are presentation only - they exist so the mockup reads as a window on this page. In the app the shell fills the browser viewport edge to edge: the sidebar runs the full height with square corners, and the content column meets the window edge with no border and no radius. Same for the fixed 1400px width, which stands in for a viewport.
Notes for implementation
The month card measures this month against one whole earlier month, set in Settings, Appearance, "Compare this month with" (11a): Last month (the default) or Same month last year. Every order dated in that calendar month counts, the same scope as Spent this month. The line names the month: "95% of 64.520 kr. in July", or "90% of 71.890 kr. in August 2025" with the year when it is last year's, the share rounded to a whole number. Category limits are never added together here or anywhere else; there is no overall monthly limit. The pace marker is unchanged (day 21 of 31 sits at 68%), and the pace words keep their rule: "ahead of pace" when the share is more than 10 points over the marker.
Past the compared month the bar stops full and stays green (state 2 below). That month is a reference, not a limit, so there is no over segment and no amber on the bar. The share keeps counting past 100%. The only amber is the "ahead of pace" word, by the same rule as before.
With nothing in the compared month the bar is drawn empty (state 4 below): a new account, an account younger than a year on Same month last year, or a month with no orders. There is no fallback to the other choice; the line names the month it looked at. The track takes the faded sp-gauge--untargeted ground, with no fill and no marker, and the line under it reads "Nothing from July to compare with yet." (or the month with its year). No pace words. The card keeps its height.
A very large reference month is not corrected. The line names its amount and month, which is enough to explain a short bar. Only the chosen month is shown, never both.
Every card is capped at a constant and none of them expand. Drafts: newest 3. Recent orders: newest 4. Categories this month: top 4 by spend. Needs attention: at most 3 alerts. The cap is a number, not a fraction of the viewport, so the page is the same height for a user with 12 orders and one with 2.400. No list on Home or Statistics shows more than one page of rows (Rows per page, never fewer than 10); Home's caps sit below it. The Statistics rule is in 4d.
The header link carries the true total - "All 248", "All 9" - and the muted label beside the card name says what the visible rows are. No Other row and no Show all: the link hands off to the order list or to Statistics, which own the full data. A card that grows with the data has stopped being a summary.
Card scopes differ and each says so. Drafts is all time, newest scan first. Recent orders is all time, newest first, and leaves drafts out. Categories this month is the current calendar month, and its total counts only categories with spend in that month. Last 8 months ends with the current month.
Alerts are ordered by severity, then recency: categories over their limit, categories near it, orders not delivered, then possible duplicate vendors. The cap of three holds, so an account with three categories over their limit shows no other row. Each kind can be switched off in Settings, Needs a look (11a); a switched-off kind is skipped and the next one moves up. Orders without a receipt is a kind too, off by default, since most orders have none. Each row links to the filtered list that resolves it - over the limit to /categories?flag=over-limit, near its limit to /categories?flag=near-limit - and the card is absent when there is nothing to say - never a card with a tick in it.
Drafts lists the orders with status Draft, newest scan first, at most 3, above Needs a look. A row is the file's first page as its picture, the vendor (the file name when no vendor was read), what is left to check and then the file name under it, the day it was scanned, and the total when one was read. With no total the amount cell is empty. What is left to check reads "2 values need a look", "4 lines" when everything was read cleanly, "Still reading", or "Nothing read" for a blank scan. A row opens the draft in the scan review, 3a step 3, with its marks. What is left to check comes first on that line, so a long file name is cut and the count never is. The card is absent when there are no drafts, like Needs a look.
An order made by hand is a draft too (state 5 below), from its first change until Create or Discard (3a). Its row has no file. The picture is the order list's thumbnail cell (2b): the first item photo, the vendor image faded, or the empty square with the image symbol. The name is the vendor, or "New order" before one is chosen. The line under it is the line count, "4 lines" or "No lines yet", and for a copy the day of the order it came from: "4 lines · Copied from 12/08". The day is the day the draft was started, and the total is the lines' total, empty with no priced line. A row opens the draft in the edit screen, unsaved, as it was left.
Orders not delivered counts the orders with status Ordered dated 14 or more days ago. It is the plain count row, not a warning: "4 orders ordered over 2 weeks ago and not delivered", "1 order ordered ..." for one; on a phone "not delivered after 2 weeks". A count of zero is no row.
Last 8 months uses the plot's bars from Statistics, at the size the card already has: 140 high, 8 between columns. A bar that a hover or focus reaches takes the soft tile and shows the Statistics readout (4a) - the month and year with its total, then the note - placed by the same rules: 8 clear of the tallest of that bar and its two neighbours, standing above the chart and the card where there is no room inside (15f), and aligned to the outer edge at either end of the chart. It never moves a bar. No category rows, since the bars are one colour.
The note reads "18 orders · click to open" for a month of this year. For a month of last year it reads only "click to open", since Home holds only that year's total spend.
Each card loads independently and shows its own skeleton at its resting height. One slow query does not delay the capture zone, which is the most common reason the page was opened and must be usable first.
Every card head is the list toolbar's height (--card-header-h, 54px), with the name centred in it. Categories this month and Last 8 months carry their names at the same height as Drafts and Recent orders beside them, and their content starts where those lists' rows start. The action beside the name is always the small outline button, and it does not change the head's height.
A zero total renders the label without a link. An empty card states the one action that fills it, in the card, at the same height it has when full.
Nothing on Home is a filter or a drill target. Clicking a category, a month bar or an order leaves Home for the screen that owns it, with the equivalent state applied. Home holds no filter state of its own, so there is nothing to restore when the user comes back.
Where each link goes
A link opens what it counts. A button or row that shows a number opens a list with exactly that many items. If it cannot, the number is not shown.
Every one is a real route, so it middle-clicks, deep-links and comes back with the browser button. The one exception is Copy an order, which opens the 3b dialog over Home.
Scan receipt · drop zone
/orders/new?source=scan - order create, scan stage. Dropping a file starts the read with that file already attached.
Copy an order
No route of its own: opens the 3b dialog over Home. Escape or Cancel closes it and stays on Home. Picking an order opens the unsaved copy, as from the create screen. Hidden when there are no orders yet.
See orders (over limit)
/orders?category=decor&month=2026-08 - the order list, filtered, filter chips visible and removable.
Near its limit
/categories?flag=near-limit - the category list filtered to the categories at or past the "Tell me early" share of their limit and not over it, the tag "Near the limit" visible and removable. It holds the same categories the row counts.
Orders not delivered
/orders?status=Ordered&to=<day> - the order list filtered to status Ordered, dated up to the cutoff day, 14 days before today (to=2026-08-07 on 21 August). It lists exactly the orders the count counts.
Merge (duplicate vendors)
/vendors?flag=possible-duplicate - the vendor list filtered to the flagged pairs, panel closed.
A draft row
/orders/452 - the draft, in the scan review (3a step 3).
All 5 (drafts)
/orders?status=Draft - the order list filtered to Draft, the status chip visible and removable. It holds the same 5 drafts the link counts.
All 248
/orders - unfiltered, default sort.
An order row
/orders/447 - the order, read mode.
All 9 (categories)
/categories?month=2026-08 - the category list for the month the card is about, so it holds the same 9 categories the link counts.
A category row
/statistics?period=2026-08&crumbs=category:decor - statistics for the month the card is about, that category as the crumb.
Statistics (last 8 months)
/statistics?period=2026 - the resting page, no crumbs.
A month bar
/statistics?period=2026&crumbs=month:2026-08 - that month as the crumb.
Sidebar saved views
/orders?view=<slug> - the order list with the view applied; the badge is that view's live count.
A row whose count is zero is text, not a link - an alert with nothing behind it does not appear at all.
