24a Invoice - the PDF
An A4 PDF made from a saved order, to hand to an employer or accountant for repayment. The vendor is the sender, the recipient is chosen when it is made, VAT at 25% is shown separately, and the text is English. Drawn at full size.
Notes for implementation
Where each value comes from. The mark is the vendor's initials and colour, as in the app. The head is the vendor's Name. Number is the order's Reference; Date is the order date; Total is the order total. From is the vendor's register name, address and CVR (5m), as they were on the order date. To is the recipient from the dialog (24c). The lines are the order's lines, with photo, quantity, unit price and line total. Note is the order's note.
Rows that are left out when empty: Shipping, Discount, Note, Att., and the From box's address and CVR. Country is printed only when it is not Denmark. A line with no photo follows the order list's thumbnail rule (Order list, 17e): the vendor's image, faded to --fade-standin (0.6), when the vendor has one; otherwise the empty photo box, a 1px dashed --line-3 edge on --surface-2 with the photo icon in --ink-6. Never a blank cell. Here Linen cushion cover (24a) shows the vendor image; Storage basket and Candle holder set (24b) are from a vendor with no image.
Prices on an order include VAT, so the PDF works backwards: VAT is 20% of the total, which is 25% of the amount without it. Here 4.079,00 kr. is 3.263,20 kr. plus 815,80 kr. VAT. Amounts are written in the account's number format.
The PDF is drawn by the app, not printed from a web page, so these sizes are exact: A4, 56 margins at 96dpi, Poppins embedded. The file is named Invoice SPENDLY-447 Indbo Møbler.pdf. It is downloaded and not kept on the order.

24b A long order, over two pages
An order of 14 lines with no Reference, a discount, a vendor with no CVR number, and no recipient. Eight lines fit on page 1; the other six continue on page 2, and the totals follow the last line.
Notes for implementation
Page 1 carries the head, the tiles, From and To. Later pages carry the head, a "Continued, Order 512" line repeating the Number tile, and the lines with their column heads repeated. The totals and the note come after the last line, on whichever page it falls; a line never splits across pages. A line that does not fit above the page foot goes whole to the next page, so the count per page follows from the lines' heights: here 8 single-line rows fit on page 1, and fewer when names wrap to two lines.
Without a Reference, Number is the order's own number, "Order 512", and the file is Invoice Order 512 Bolia.pdf. Every order has a number, counted per account from 1. It is printed on the invoice only: the order page, the order list and search do not show it.
With no recipient, To prints the account's name and email, as they are in Settings at the time the PDF is made. Nothing else goes in the box.
A discount is a row after the lines, shown when the order has one, as a negative amount. VAT is worked out from the total after the discount.
Page numbers are "Page 1 of 2" in the foot of every page.

24c Make invoice - the dialog
Opened from Make invoice in the order row and card menus (2b, 8a) and on the order page (1a). One choice, the recipient, with the page beside it as it will download.
Notes for implementation
One recipient is remembered, per account. The recipient is usually the user, typed once. The dialog opens with the recipient of the last downloaded invoice filled in, and edits are made in place; Download PDF saves them as the new remembered recipient, so a second invoice is one click. The first invoice opens with the fields empty. Every field is optional: with all of them empty, To prints the account's name and email (24b).
One view, the vendor's fields. Name, Country, CVR, Address, Att., in the order and with the behaviour of the vendor's own fields (5m). CVR shows when Country is Denmark; outside Denmark it is VAT number, printed as typed and not looked up (24d). Country is printed only when it is not Denmark. Att. is printed under the name.
Eight digits in CVR look the company up at once. This lookup is live, unlike the vendor's, because the answer is needed now. Found, Address becomes the register's name and address with "From the CVR register." under it, and Name is filled with the register name when it was empty (second specimen). The invoice prints the register name, address and CVR number. Clearing CVR turns Address back into a field, holding what the register gave. There is no search by name: a person's name would only spend lookups.
The lookup's other states, each as the help line under CVR, with Address staying a field: while it runs, "Looking up…"; no match, "No company has CVR 87654321 in the register. Check the number."; no answer from the register, "The company could not be looked up. Try again, or type the address."; the day's 50 free lookups used (5m), "Company lookup is not available until tomorrow. Type the address instead." A remembered company needs no new lookup.
The preview is the PDF at 40% and redraws as the fields change. Below 760 the preview goes and the dialog is the plain 420 dialog (sp-dialog--preview, c13). This is the dialog the phone gets, opened from the order page's Make invoice (1a), since the phone's order list has no row menu.
Download PDF downloads the file and closes the dialog. There is no toast; the browser's download is the confirmation. Cancel and Escape close it with nothing made.

24d Vendor without an address, recipient typed
The same dialog for a vendor with neither an address nor a CVR number, and a remembered recipient outside Denmark.
Notes for implementation
The invoice is made anyway, with the vendor's Name alone in From. The alert says so above the fields; Add them closes the dialog and opens the vendor's edit page (5c) in the same tab, as every link in the app does. Back on the order, Make invoice opens the dialog again with what was saved.
The alert shows for a missing address or a missing CVR number, naming what is missing. A vendor outside Denmark with an address and a VAT number has nothing missing.
A CVR number the register does not know is not printed. The alert reads "The CVR number of Flying Tiger is not in the register", with the link "Check the number", which closes the dialog and opens the vendor's edit page in the same tab, as Add them does.
A company that has ceased trading (5m) gets no alert. The invoice prints its last address and CVR number, since an order placed while it traded is still a real invoice.
The fields as in 24c, every one optional. With Country Sweden, CVR is VAT number, left empty here, and Country is printed because it is not Denmark.
