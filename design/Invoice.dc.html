24a Invoice - the PDF
An A4 PDF made from a saved order, to hand to an employer or accountant for repayment. The vendor is the sender, the recipient is chosen when it is made, VAT at 25% is shown separately, and the text is English. Drawn at full size.
Notes for implementation
Where each value comes from. The mark is the vendor's initials and colour, as in the app. The head is the vendor's Name. Number is the order's Reference; Date is the order date; Total is the order total. From is the vendor's register name, address and CVR (5m), as they were on the order date. To is the recipient from the dialog (24c). The lines are the order's lines, with photo, quantity, unit price and line total. Note is the order's note.
Rows that are left out when empty: Shipping, Discount, Note, Att., and the From box's address and CVR. Country is printed only when it is not Denmark. A line with no photo leaves the photo cell empty.
Prices on an order include VAT, so the PDF works backwards: VAT is 20% of the total, which is 25% of the amount without it. Here 4.079,00 kr. is 3.263,20 kr. plus 815,80 kr. VAT. Amounts are written in the account's number format.
The PDF is drawn by the app, not printed from a web page, so these sizes are exact: A4, 56 margins at 96dpi, Poppins embedded. The file is named Invoice SPENDLY-447 Indbo Møbler.pdf. It is downloaded and not kept on the order.

24b A long order, over two pages
An order of 14 lines with no Reference, a discount, and a vendor with no CVR number. The lines continue on page 2; the totals follow the last line.
Notes for implementation
Page 1 carries the head, the tiles, From and To. Later pages carry the head, a "Continued, Order 512" line repeating the Number tile, and the lines with their column heads repeated. The totals and the note come after the last line, on whichever page it falls; a line never splits across pages.
Without a Reference, Number is the order's own number, "Order 512", and the file is Invoice Order 512 Bolia.pdf.
A discount is a row after the lines, shown when the order has one, as a negative amount. VAT is worked out from the total after the discount.
Page numbers are "Page 1 of 2" in the foot of every page.

24c Make invoice - the dialog
Opened from Make invoice in the order row and card menus (2b, 8a). One choice, the recipient, with the page beside it as it will download.
Notes for implementation
The recipient is remembered. The dialog opens with the last recipient used, so a second invoice is one click. It is kept per account, not per vendor.
Recipient takes a CVR number or a company name. Eight digits look it up at once; a name looks up on Enter. This lookup is live, unlike the vendor's (5m), because the answer is needed now. The company found shows under the field from the CVR register and goes on the invoice as found. With no match the help line reads "No Danish company found. Type the address instead." and the field stays.
When the day's 50 free lookups are used (5m), the help line reads "Company lookup is not available until tomorrow. Type the address instead." and the typed fields open. A recipient found earlier is remembered and needs no lookup, and a name is looked up on Enter rather than as it is typed, so a search costs one lookup.
Type the address instead swaps the lookup for Company, Address and Country fields, for a recipient outside Denmark or without a CVR number (24d). Look up instead swaps back. What was typed is remembered the same way.
Att. is optional and printed under the company name.
The preview is the PDF at 40% and redraws as the fields change. Below 760 the preview goes and the dialog is the plain 420 dialog (sp-dialog--preview, c13).
Download PDF downloads the file and closes the dialog. There is no toast; the browser's download is the confirmation. Cancel and Escape close it with nothing made.

24d Vendor without an address, recipient typed
The same dialog for a vendor with neither an address nor a CVR number, and a recipient outside Denmark typed by hand.
Notes for implementation
The invoice is made anyway, with the vendor's Name alone in From. The alert says so above the fields; Add them opens the vendor's edit page (5c) in a new tab, and the preview picks up what is saved there when the dialog is next opened.
The alert shows for a missing address or a missing CVR number, naming what is missing. A vendor outside Denmark with an address and a VAT number has nothing missing.
Typed fields: Company is required for Download PDF; Address, Country and Att. are optional. Country defaults to Denmark and is printed only when it is not.
