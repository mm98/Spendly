7a Every screen on a phone
One frame per page, at the width .sp-phone sets - 375, the narrowest phone that matters, labelled with the desktop section it corresponds to. This is the lookup: if a screen is not here, it uses the shell from 9h and the rules in 15b and 18a without further changes.
NOTES FOR IMPLEMENTATION
The order list's search ends in the Ask button, inside the field at its drawn 30px with a 44px press area. Ask mode, its states and the keys are drawn and noted in Order list 2b.
Ask is a row in More, above Settings, opening its page (Ask 24a): the touch top bar with Back, the title and a menu holding Chats, Files, Email this chat and New chat, in that order (Ask 24c); the conversation; and the dock pinned above the tab bar (.sp-chat__dock--bar) with the field and Send (Stop while an answer comes). The frame is drawn inside .sp-touch, so its controls show at the touch height. The row and the page are absent when the server has no assistant set up.
What Ask can do is the same button at the field's right edge, opening the same parts in the bottom sheet, with the footnote "Files are kept for 30 days" over Close, a block button under them; a tap on the scrim or a drag down also closes it (Ask 24c). An example question closes the sheet and puts the question in the field, not sent; the keyboard does not open until the reader taps the field.
An answer with a file or a chart takes the full column, as one with a table does (Ask 24d). Twelve bars fit the bubble with no sideways scroll; every second tick is left empty where two would touch, and a tap on a bar opens its readout, a tap elsewhere closes it. Chats (24f) and Files (24e) open from the menu as their own pages with Back: the same rows and buttons at the touch height, the search above the chats. On a file row the title takes two lines beside Open chat, Download and Delete.
The confirm banner on Ask keeps the phone gutter: Ask's body has no padding on a phone, so the banner (the warning row, 20e) sits --phone-gutter from the screen's edges and above the thread, as on every other phone page.
The shell is identical on every screen. Search-only top bar, four-destination tab bar, the New order FAB on Home and the order list. On the places, the top bar is search only and never gains a title or a hamburger. A screen one level down - a record's page, create and edit, the scan review - swaps it for Back and a title, as the order and vendor frames show (Navigation shell, top bar note).
The floating action is New order, not the camera - it opens step 1 of 3a: the receipt zone, an empty order, a copy of an order. It is the only floating action in the app, and the receipt zone leads because the phone is the camera. It does not open the camera directly.
It is on Home and Orders, and nowhere else. An order is what it creates, so it appears only where orders are the subject. Vendors and Categories create their own records from the + in their list head; Statistics and Settings create nothing; and any screen carrying a save bar, a sheet, a dialog or the image viewer hides it while that is open. The full rule is in 9h.
The tab bar is on every place and on no task. It stays on the four destinations and on everything inside them - a list, an overview, a drilled slice, More and what More opens - so the same four are always one tap away. It is absent while a task owns the screen: creating or editing a record, where the save bar takes the bottom; the scan review; full-screen search; the image viewer; and under a sheet or a dialog. It comes back when the task ends, by saving, cancelling or dismissing. No screen shows a tab bar and a save bar at once. The full rule is in 9h.
On a device with a home bar band, the tab bar's height takes the band in, and its items sit 12 lower than centred, so they do not float over the empty band. .sp-tabbar--band, drawn in Components.
Tapping search opens the palette over the whole screen - the same palette as 9d, full bleed, its search row standing at the top bar's own height and gutter so the field does not move. The field keeps what was typed and carries a clear control at its right edge, where the keyboard hint sits on desktop. Rows keep the name and one line of detail and drop the amount and the key hints: 375px has room for what the row is, not for everything the row could say. It is drawn in 7b.
More is a list of the areas the tab bar has no room for - the account, Budget, Vendors, Categories, Bookmarks, saved views and Settings, in the sidebar's order. It is the fourth tab, so it keeps the tab bar and shows More as current; everything it opens keeps More current too, which is why the Vendors, Categories and Settings frames below show it that way. The theme is under Settings, in Appearance.
Appearance on a phone holds Colour scheme under Theme: the tiles from 11a, three to a row, a tap choosing one. The Appearance row in Settings shows no value.
A create or edit screen is the order edit frame with a different form. Back control and title in the bar, cards of fields in the scroll, the save bar at the bottom - 5b is drawn here to show it; 5c, 5e and 5f are the same frame.
Rows become cards, and cards drop fields rather than shrinking them. An order card is the card from 2i: vendor and amount on line one, then the date and items with the saving under the amount. The reference, the status and the category are not on the card; the order page shows them. Everything else lives one level deeper.
Filters and sort become two controls above the list, not a facet rail. Filters opens the bottom sheet in 7b and carries a count when any are applied.
Anything that creates or edits gets a sticky save bar at the bottom of the content, clear of the safe-area inset. It takes the tab bar's place rather than sitting above it - the screen is a task until it is saved or cancelled. The primary reads Create on a new record and Save on an existing one, and the page title already says which record it is.
With the period on Month, Spend by month becomes Spend by day (Statistics · one month): 31 bars, labelled every 6 days from the first to the last: 1, 7, 13, 19, 25 and 31. The rule in c14 picks 6 at this width; on desktop, with more room, it picks 2 (4g), as on desktop (4g). The chosen day's label shows in its tooltip.
These frames are drawn at 375px, but the rule is the 640px threshold in 9h - not this device.
Budget shows one month at a time. The twelve-month strip from 21a does not fit, so the window control steps through the months and the halves become a single well. Year, Show income and Edit share one bar. The year is the Combobox with its field box (sp-combobox--grow), taking the width the two buttons leave. Edit is the desktop bar's Edit button, unchanged.
There is no whole year on a phone. Below 640px the Months / Whole year switch is not drawn and Budget always shows Months; a link to the whole year opens Months on the month it is now. 21b is a desktop view: sixteen columns of amounts cannot be read or typed into at this width.
Amounts are typed one month at a time. Each amount line is sp-sum--edits, as on desktop; tapping it turns the amount into the NumberField in place (sp-input--cell), with the decimal keyboard. Return saves and moves to the next amount. Electricity is drawn mid-edit. Inside sp-touch the line holding the field keeps the 48px touch height.
Editing a budget row keeps its desktop behaviour (21c): the row editor opens in place of the row.
Checking a bank statement is not offered on a phone (21g). Below 640px the button is not drawn.
Bookmarks is the 22a card list in one column - the card grid already drops to one column below 572px. Filters opens the bottom sheet from 7b holding Category and Open / Archived; + in the list head is New bookmark, as on Vendors. New and edit bookmark are the create frame shown for 5b.
Status email on a phone is a row in the Settings list, right after Two-step sign-in, with the chevron and no value. It opens its own page, /settings/status-email, holding the Status email card from Account and profile (11a) unchanged: the Weekly and Monthly checkboxes save as they are tapped, and Send a test email sends the test exactly as on desktop, Sending... while it goes, then the toast. Back returns to the list. On desktop the address still opens Account and profile.
Home on a phone has no monthly summary, and nothing replaces it. Needs a look already covers the limits.
"N over their limit" opens /categories?flag=over-limit: Categories cut to the categories over their limit, each in its own current month or year (5d).
"N not delivered after 2 weeks" opens /orders?status=Ordered&to=<day>, as on Home (10a): orders with status Ordered dated 14 or more days ago.
Recent orders carries "All 248" in its head, the same text link and true total as the desktop card, opening the order list (2b). The muted label says the card shows the newest 3. Drafts are not in it.
Drafts sits under Needs a look, the order of the desktop Home (10a), and directly under This month when Needs a look is absent: newest 3, "All 5" in its head opening /orders?status=Draft. A row is the file's picture, the vendor or the file name, the scan day and what is left to check, and the total when one was read. A row opens the draft in Check the reading (7b), Back keeping it. Absent when there are no drafts. An order made by hand is a row too, with the order list's thumbnail cell, the vendor or "New order", and the day it was started with its line count: "21/08 · 4 lines". It opens in the edit screen.
A card head's action is the small outline button, "All 5" and "All 248" as everywhere else, at its 34px box, with the 48px touch height as an invisible area above and below it. An icon button in a card head (Totals' Duplicate, the close button on Home's month summary) does the same: its 32px box, with a 48px press area around it, so it does not crowd the card's top edge. Every card head is the list toolbar's height (--card-header-h) with the name centred in it, so Drafts and Recent orders have the same head height as This month and Needs a look, and every card's name sits the same distance below its top edge, with or without a button. It holds in touch mode and below 640px.
A bookmark card keeps its desktop head on a phone. Both chips in the head stay at the chip's own height and sit level; the first, which acts, gets its 48px touch target as an invisible area above and below it, so the head does not grow. Every bookmark card is the same height, with two categories, one or none. The Bookmarks frame is drawn in touch mode to show it: Linen bedding set has two categories, Outline Sofa none.
The controls here are drawn at their desktop heights. The library has a touch mode that raises a control to 48px, and a real build applies it on every phone screen; these frames show it only where the control's shape changes with it, as the period presets do. Read the heights from Components c0 (--control-h-touch), not off these frames.

Notes for implementation
On a phone the Statistics period is one select: 30 days, Week, Month, Quarter, Year, Custom, with the window it resolves to beside the name ("Year · 2026"). It is the same six presets as the desktop row (4a).
Arrows either side of the select step the period by its own length: the month before, the quarter before, the year before. Later is disabled on the period that holds today. On Custom both are disabled, since a range of its own has no next or previous (4f). The control is sp-window--select, the window from 21a with the select in its middle.
Include N left-out orders sits under the period select, the same switch and rules as the desktop period row (4a): shown only when the period holds a left-out order, off by default, not remembered.
The order list keeps its own search field on a phone, above Filters and Sort. It filters the list; the top bar's search is the palette, which finds records but does not filter.
Applied filters wrap, one chip each, followed by Clear all - the filter row of c12.
Reset in the sheet's header is a text button (sp-btn--text, small), in the button's primary, like Cancel in the search screen; it clears every filter and keeps the sheet open. Show 39 more expands that facet's list in place inside the sheet.
Show 18 orders applies the filters and closes the sheet. The label is the live result count; with no results it reads "No orders match" and is disabled.
Cancel closes the full-screen search and returns to the screen it was opened from, as it was. It is the only way out: the tab bar is absent while the search is open (9h). The clear button inside the field empties the query and keeps the search open.
