7a Every screen on a phone
One frame per page, at 390 × 780, labelled with the desktop section it corresponds to. This is the lookup: if a screen is not here, it uses the shell from 9h and the rules in 15b and 18a without further changes.
NOTES FOR IMPLEMENTATION
The shell is identical on every screen. Search-only top bar, four-destination tab bar, the camera FAB on Home and the order list. No screen adds a title, a hamburger or a back arrow to the top bar — a record page puts its own back control in the content, as the order edit frame shows.
Rows become cards, and cards drop fields rather than shrinking them. An order card carries vendor and amount on line one, reference beneath, then date, category and status. Everything else lives one level deeper.
Filters and sort become two controls above the list, not a facet rail. Filters opens the bottom sheet in 7b and carries a count when any are applied.
Anything that edits gets a sticky save bar at the bottom of the content, above the tab bar and clear of the safe-area inset.
These frames are drawn at 390px, but the rule is the 640px threshold in 9h — not this device.
