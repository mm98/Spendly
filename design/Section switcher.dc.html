Notes for implementation
Links, not tabs: the Section nav (c35), as in Settings and import 11a, the open section aria-current="page". Each section is its own view at its own URL (#statuses); only the open section is in the page, as a section labelled by its heading.
The shell is 11a's (Settings and import 11a): the sidebar with Settings current, the top bar, and the section nav in 11a's place beside the pane. The pane fills the width beside the nav, without 11a's 640 cap.
Picking a row replaces the content and moves focus to the section's heading. Nothing scrolls: the content starts at the top of the view, under general information. A section taller than the view grows the page, as any page does.
Find in the page reaches only the open section. Settings in other sections are reached through the nav.
On a phone the nav becomes a row of chips under the top bar that scrolls sideways; picking one opens that section the same way.
