25 Settings - one group at a time, as a wide form
Settings in five groups, one shown at a time, each setting one row: what it is and what it does at the left, the control at the right. The column uses the full width beside the sidebar, and every group fits on a laptop screen.
Notes for implementation
The five groups: Account (25a), Sign-in and security (25b), Preferences (25c), Notifications (25d), Data (25e). Each is its own address (/settings/preferences); /settings opens Account.
The first card moves between groups. Its head reads SETTINGS, as every page names itself in a card head, and it holds the five groups on the Segmented in its fill variant (c5); the open group is the selected one. Picking one opens that group's address. The palette (9d) also finds each setting by name and opens its group at it.
A form row (.st-form-row) is a two-column grid: label and help in the left column, the control in the right at 1.2 times its width. The two columns stay aligned down the whole group, so a group reads as one form. A row holding a list (passkeys, tokens, statuses, recently deleted) starts both columns at the top. Rows inside a card are separated by the card's rule.
Saving: the save bar names the group and sits at the foot of the column, as drawn in 25c; it appears only with unsaved changes. Leaving the group with unsaved changes asks first with the confirm dialog. Rows that act at once (passkeys, tokens, status email, restore, the buttons) do not touch the bar, as today.
Below 900px the rows stack, label over control (25f).

On a phone the five groups do not fit across, so the Settings card lists them as rows with what each is set to; a row opens its group, whose first card carries the group's name before its own (PREFERENCES · APPEARANCE) and All settings to go back. Settings is reached from More, as today.
