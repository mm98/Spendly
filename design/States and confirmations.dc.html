6c Validation
Validation happens where the value is, on leaving the field, and Save stays clickable — pressing it moves focus to the first problem instead of greying out with no explanation. Messages say what to do, not what went wrong. Amber is a warning you may pass; red blocks the save.

6d Confirmations
Reversible things get a toast with Undo and no dialog. A dialog appears only when the effect reaches beyond what you can see — deleting an order with attachments, merging two vendors, discarding a scan. Each one names the consequence in numbers and puts the destructive verb on the button, never “OK”.
