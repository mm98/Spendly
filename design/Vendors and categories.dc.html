5b Categories
Categories carry the monthly limit, so the list is the budget overview: limit, this month against it, and the twelve-month average that tells you whether the limit is realistic. The panel is the category form — name, icon, colour and the limit, which is what Statistics reads. Icon and colour travel together: the icon is picked from a fixed set and rendered in the category colour, so chips, facets and charts stay recognisable.
