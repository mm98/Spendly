## A long dialog on the phone opens a little scrolled down

Found 2026-10-17, on the phone (iPhone 15), in Make an invoice when it opens on the typed address
(Company, Address, Country, Att.). The sheet is taller than the screen and opens scrolled by about
30 pixels, so the top half of the title "Make an invoice" is cut off. The dialog first puts focus on
its main button at the bottom and then on the first field, and the sheet keeps part of that scroll.
The shorter look-up form fits and opens at the top.
